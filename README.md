# LINKLY: Annual Partner Business Plan and AI Value-Selling Kit (Sample)

A two-page sample plan written by a partner manager at a fictional enterprise cloud ERP and business AI vendor. It covers a portfolio of six fictional partners and focuses on LINKLY, a mid-sized IT solution partner that resells and implements the vendor's software.

- [한국어 PDF (Korean)](LINKLY_Partner_Plan_KR.pdf)

## What's inside
1. Partner portfolio: six partners scored on six weighted criteria, with time allocated by remaining opportunity rather than by score
2. LINKLY diagnosis: where the partner is held back (AI-certified staff, pipeline coverage)
3. Three-year business plan and channel rules: vendor subscription revenue vs. partner services revenue, deal registration and co-selling, partner-sourced pipeline, operating rhythm
4. AI first-adoption pipeline for FY1, with a low-case range and fallback actions
5. AI value-selling kit: three customer workflows, how each is measured in a PoC, and the rule of separating what an AI dashboard credits from what actually increased
6. Competitive objections, answers, and the evidence to bring
7. 90-day plan showing what I do and who I work with on the vendor and partner side
8. How each part maps to my own experience (Shopee 2020–2024, PhD research)

LINKLY, partners B to F, the vendor, and all numbers and dates are fictional. Not affiliated with SAP or any real company, and no product names or internal information are used.
